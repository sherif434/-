## 2026-10-30 - Arabic ARIA labels for quantity controls
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like quantity increment/decrement buttons) in localized RTL applications require clear localized `aria-label` attributes in the target language (Arabic) so screen reader users understand the button action.
**Action:** When creating or modifying icon-only buttons in Eelaf Shop, always add Arabic `aria-label` attributes (e.g. `aria-label="إنقاص الكمية"` or `aria-label="زيادة الكمية"`).
